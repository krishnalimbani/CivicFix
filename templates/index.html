How CivicFix Works

Report a civic issue in three easy steps and track its resolution

01

Report the Issue

Fill out the simple form with the issue category, description, location, and an optional photo.

02

Get Your Report ID

Receive a unique Report ID instantly. Use it anytime to track the real-time status of your complaint.

03

Issue Gets Resolved

Authorities review and resolve the issue. You can track progress from Submitted to Resolved.
